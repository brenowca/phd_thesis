\caption{Measurement setup. Clean images and perturbed images go through the same frozen
ViT-B/16. At each probed block, the MLP output is mean-pooled over the 197 tokens. The
pooled activation feeds three paths: the displacement $\|\Delta a\|$, the SAE trained on clean
activations, and the linear probe. The probe reads $a$ directly or the SAE outputs
$z$, $\hat a$ and $r$. The linear head $W$ gives the label and the split of the head-input displacement
$\Delta h$ into $\Delta h_\parallel$ and $\Delta h_\perp$.}
